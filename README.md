# Beverage Brand Performance Dashboard

## Introduction
This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of beverage brands' performance across various categories and regions.
It helps stakeholders understand sales trends, revenue distribution, and customer preferences.

# Project Tasks
- Sales Insights: Evaluate discount impact, shipping preferences, and loyal customers.
- Visualization: Create charts to highlight key insights.
- Recommendations: Suggest strategies based on findings to boost sales and customer engagement.

# Visualizations:
  - Bar charts for Regoin/Brand Vs Operating profit.
  - Pie charts for market share Brand.
  - Scatter plot for Total sales vs operating profit AND Total sales vs unit sold.

## Insights
- Coca-Cola has the highest sales in all regions compared to other beverage brands.
- Walmart is the Leading Retailer
- Dasani Water and Powerade show high total sales despite lower units sold, likely due to premium pricing or larger packaging driving higher revenue per unit.

 ## Recommendations 
 ### 1- Coca-Cola has the highest sales in all regions compared to other beverage brands.
 - 1 **Enhance Brand Awareness**: Increase marketing efforts and advertising campaigns to boost brand visibility, especially in regions where Coca-Cola is dominant.

-  2  **Product Diversification**: Introduce new flavors or healthier options to cater to a wider audience and create a unique value proposition.

- 3 **Targeted Promotions**: Implement region-specific promotions, discounts, or loyalty programs to encourage consumer switching from Coca-Cola to your brand.

### 2- Walmart is the Leading Retailer

 - 1 **Focus on Competitive Pricing**: Offer value-based pricing strategies to attract price-conscious customers while maintaining quality and service.

 - 2 **Strengthen Online Presence**: Invest in e-commerce and improve the online shopping experience to compete with Walmart's strong omnichannel approach.

 - 3 **Offer Unique Products or Services**: Differentiate by offering exclusive products, better customer service, or unique in-store experiences to attract loyal customers.

### 3- ### Recommendations for Brands Similar to Dasani Water and Powerade:

1. **Leverage Premium Pricing**: 
   - Consider increasing the price of certain products or offering premium variants (e.g., larger packaging, enhanced features) to increase total sales per unit.

2. **Market Differentiation**: 
   - Focus on branding and positioning to emphasize the value, quality, or exclusivity of the product, justifying higher pricing.

3. **Expand Distribution**: 
   - Ensure that premium products are accessible in regions with a higher willingness to pay, targeting niche markets like fitness enthusiasts or health-conscious consumers.
